How RideNest works

For renters

  1. Search by city, vehicle type and your pickup/drop time.
  2. Book — the price (rent + 5% service fee + refundable deposit) is shown before you confirm.
  3. Sign the digital rental agreement. The owner signs too.
  4. Pick up once both signatures are in; the owner marks the handover.
  5. Return on time, then rate your experience.

For owners

  1. List your car or bike with hourly and daily prices, deposit and pickup spot.
  2. Get bookings — overlapping bookings are blocked automatically.
  3. Countersign the agreement, hand over the keys, mark pickup and return.
  4. Track earnings by vehicle and month in your dashboard.

About the digital agreement

Every booking creates an agreement containing both parties, the vehicle, the rental window, the price breakdown and ten standard clauses. Signatures are timestamped and bound to a SHA-256 fingerprint of the agreement text, so any later edit would be detected. Either party can print it or download a JSON copy.
